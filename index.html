Why Millipedes Matter?

Millipedes are among the most important decomposers in forest ecosystems, breaking down leaf litter and recycling nutrients back into the soil. Nepal's diverse topography creates unique habitats for these fascinating creatures.

Our research aims to document and understand the millipede biodiversity of Nepal, with special focus on endemic species and their conservation status.

  • Essential decomposers of forest ecosystems
  • Indicators of soil health and habitat quality
  • Nepal hosts unique and endemic species
  • Ongoing research and new discoveries
